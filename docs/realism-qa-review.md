# Financial operations review

This review records what the provider application enforces and where its operating model remains incomplete. The records are fixed test data; no route executes an external financial transaction.

| Service | Verified in this pass | Remaining gap |
|---|---|---|
| Employee Stock Ownership | Payroll lines reconcile to effective contribution elections. The employer incentive is calculated from a dated plan rule and a batch-to-rule link is retained. An operator can inspect rejected payroll lines and post an evidenced correction into a new batch; the original rejected batch remains and the case resolves. Toyota can hold fractional beneficial association units, while transfer requests are limited to whole shares. The recipient custody account must belong to the employee and tenant, be active, and support the security. Completion requires a unique settlement confirmation and posts matching stock-out/custody-in events under one correlation ID. Association dividends no longer include custody cash. | The opening stock and custody positions are separately sourced; the proposed 149.75-to-49.75 / 100-share connected opening scenario is not seeded. Provider payroll and purchase forms exist, while association dividend posting and bank reconciliation are not fully modeled. |
| Corporate Pension | Account units and dated NAVs determine the displayed holdings. An allocation draft changes future contributions only. A separate switch draft is permissioned, idempotent, employee scoped, and leaves both current positions and future allocations unchanged. | Switch execution and its pricing/settlement stages are absent. Institution-level recordkeeper, trustee, and asset-manager roles, stale NAV checks, and contribution remittance reconciliation need dedicated records. |
| Financial Wellness | Benefit rules retain source and effective date. Stock and pension rule explanations require the employee's underlying service permissions and enabled tenant policy. The combined benefit comparison requires both services. | Content revisions and approval history, seminar registration/attendance, employer-specific learning pathways, and privacy-protected aggregate reports are not complete. |
| Trust & Custody | Custody inventory is separate from association beneficial interests. A confirmed transfer links one stock debit to one custody credit and a settlement confirmation record. Corporate-action entitlement uses position quantity as of the record date. | Position lots, external transfer acknowledgements, settlement-failure recovery, multi-instrument reconciliation, and broader statement generation remain outstanding. The current sample corporate action is on the same security as the association and should be broadened before using it as an independent-custody example. |
| Equity Compensation | Grant, vesting, release, option exercise draft, and custody delivery are separate states. An awarded RSU is not counted as delivered custody stock. | PSU performance certification, termination/forfeiture policy variants, tax withholding, and reporting jobs are not modeled end to end. |
| Identity and disclosure | Employee routes derive tenant and employee from a signed token, require service eligibility, and reject inactive employees or tenants. A tenant-scoped provider operator can use only granted service operations. A corporate plan administrator can retrieve aggregate counts and agreement references without employee details. Wellness cannot be used to bypass stock or pension service policy. Policy denials and accepted operations are audited. | A deployed integration needs delegated employee identity, consent lifecycle, key rotation, and operational authentication controls. Aggregate corporate reporting does not yet include a broader privacy-thresholded metrics catalog. |

## Connected-record quality gate

For newly posted stock payroll, the amount, incentive, effective rule, employee election, dated batch, and resulting journal events can be traced. For newly completed association transfers, the request, approved recipient account, settlement confirmation, stock debit, and custody credit share one correlation reference. The pack's exact proposed opening scenario is still a data milestone. Existing opening positions have distinct source references and must not be described as one movement or added together as a single ownership figure.

## Validation

`backend/tests/test_realism_qa.py` covers recipient-account ownership and status, rule-derived incentives, fractional beneficial interests and whole-share transfer limits, pension switch isolation, cross-service privacy, employer and inactive-employee denial, and inactive-tenant denial. The existing stock and custody tests cover settlement confirmation and matched ledger posting. The complete backend suite, frontend build, HTTP smoke, and browser smoke should be rerun after changes.
